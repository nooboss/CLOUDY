G.E7.30. Korea: Buddha's Enlightenment Day (Seongdo-jaeil)
Eighth day of the twelfth lunar month

Korea's Buddha's Enlightenment Day (Korean: Seongdo-jaeil, Buddha's Enlightenment Day) commemorates the night of the Buddha's awakening beneath the Bodhi tree on the eighth day of the twelfth lunar month — the same date as Vietnam (core article G.E7.3) and China (article G.E7.18).

1. The observance in Korea

On the eighth day of the twelfth lunar month, Korean temples hold sutra chanting, prostrations, and precept observance. The Zen (Seon) tradition of the Jogye Order (introduced in article G.E7.27) emphasises this day as an occasion for Dharma interviews (Korean: in-ga): the student presents their understanding of the teaching, and the teacher confirms or corrects it — a characteristic Zen way of commemorating, since Enlightenment Day is the day wisdom shone forth (article G.B2).

2. Three layers for understanding Korea's Enlightenment Day

  • Foundational meaning: the night of awakening — realising the Four Noble Truths (articles G.B2, G.C1) — the same core as G.E7.
  • Shared practice: sutra chanting, prostrations, precept observance; in the Zen school: Dharma interviews.
  • Custom: Korean monasteries join this day with the winter retreat (dong-angeo — explained in article G.E7.28) — the intensive end-of-year cultivation season.

3. What newcomers should know

  • The Four Noble Truths (Pali: cattāri ariya-saccāni, the Four Noble Truths) — the content of the night of awakening — see articles G.C1 and G.B2.
  • For the full core article, see G.E7.
  • For the spirit of learning without anxiety about mistakes, see G.E6.

4. Summary — closing the Korean calendar group

  • Korea's Enlightenment Day falls on the eighth day of the twelfth lunar month; its Korean name is Seongdo-jaeil.
  • The Korean calendar group (G.E7.27 → G.E7.30) comprises four dates in the Korean lunar calendar: Buddha's Birthday (eighth day of the fourth month — a national holiday), Renunciation (eighth day of the second month), Nirvana (fifteenth day of the second month), Enlightenment (eighth day of the twelfth month).

Next: the following article — G.E7.31 — opens the Japanese calendar group (G.E7.31 → G.E7.36) — the only group dated by the Gregorian calendar. The first article is Japan's Nirvana Observance (Nehan-e), on 15 February.

Glossary:

  • Seongdo-jaeil (Korean, Buddha's Enlightenment Day): the Korean name for the Buddha's enlightenment day.
  • Dharma interview (Korean: in-ga): the session in which a student presents their understanding of the teaching before a teacher — a Zen custom.
  • Four Noble Truths (Pali: cattāri ariya-saccāni): the four foundational truths the Buddha taught after awakening — see article G.C1.

Source notes
