G.E7.8. Vietnam: Medicine Buddha Commemoration Day
Thirtieth day of the ninth lunar month

This observance honours Bhaiṣajyaguru, the Medicine Buddha, traditionally kept in Vietnam on the thirtieth day of the ninth lunar month. He represents healing, clear discernment, and the removal of causes of suffering, not a substitute for medical care.

1. Who is the Medicine Buddha?

Bhaiṣajyaguru is the Buddha of the eastern Pure Land, traditionally portrayed with a healing bowl or medicine jar. The Medicine Buddha Sutra describes his vows to relieve suffering and support beings on the path. The blue image often used in art is symbolic, not a claim about a physical medicine.

2. Why this date?

The thirtieth day of the ninth lunar month is a Vietnamese devotional convention, not an independently established historical birthday. Where a lunar month has only twenty-nine days, temples follow their own calendar arrangements.

Healing in Buddhist meaning

Buddhist healing includes care for body and mind, compassion for the ill, ethical conduct, and wisdom that addresses greed, hatred, and delusion. Prayers and chanting can support calm and courage, but they do not replace doctors, treatment, or responsible health decisions.

What newcomers should know

  • Services may include sutra recitation, the Medicine Buddha mantra, offerings, and dedication of merit for the ill.
  • Seek appropriate medical care for illness; Buddhist practice can accompany—not replace—it.
  • Offerings are voluntary; caring for someone who is ill is itself a fitting practice of compassion.

Summary and next steps

  • The day honours Bhaiṣajyaguru on the thirtieth day of the ninth lunar month in Vietnam.
  • It frames healing as compassionate care and transformation, never as a replacement for medicine.

Glossary:

  • Bhaiṣajyaguru: the Medicine Buddha.
  • Medicine Buddha Sutra: the Mahāyāna sutra associated with Bhaiṣajyaguru and his vows.
  • Dedication of merit: dedicating wholesome results for the welfare of others.

Source notes

  • Bhaiṣajyaguru's vows are presented in the Medicine Buddha Sutra.
  • The Vietnamese date and service forms are customary; health decisions require appropriate professional care.
