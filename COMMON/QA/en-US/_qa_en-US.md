# Buddhist Q&A Article List (English)

## Q1.A. Food and Karma

Q.A1. Does eating meat create the karma of killing?
Q.A2. If a person eats a vegetarian diet but is still prone to anger, do they really create less karma?
Q.A3. Does killing an animal create the same karma as killing a human being?

## Q1.B. Death and the Unseen World

Q.B1. Where does a person actually go after death?
Q.B2. Does the soul exist after death?
Q.B3. Is buddha-nature the same thing as a soul?
Q.B4. If there is no soul, what carries over from one life to the next?
Q.B5. Can the dead see the loved ones they left behind?
Q.B6. Can the deceased hear what the living say to them?
Q.B7. Is someone who dies suddenly more likely to become a "wandering spirit"?
Q.B8. Can a spirit possess the body of a living person?

## Q1.C. Nirvana

Q.C1. Is nirvana the same as "death"?
Q.C2. Is nirvana a place, or a state of mind?
Q.C3. Did the Buddha still exist after entering final nirvana (parinirvana)?
Q.C4. Once nirvana is attained, does one continue to exist, or cease entirely?

## Q1.D. Karma and Cause and Effect

Q.D1. Does karma really determine a person's fate?
Q.D2. Is all suffering in life caused by one's own karma?
Q.D3. Can karma created in the past be changed?
Q.D4. If karma determines everything, is there still any point in making an effort?
Q.D5. If there is cause and effect, who is the one judging a person?

## Q1.E. Free Will and Non-Self

Q.E1. If everything arises from conditions, do people really have free will?
Q.E2. If the "self" does not exist, then who is suffering?
Q.E3. If Buddhism teaches "non-self," then who is it that gets reborn?
Q.E4. Can a person become fully awakened while still alive?

## Q1.F. Buddhism and Theology

Q.F1. From a Buddhist point of view, do demons and evil spirits really exist?
Q.F2. Does Buddhism believe in a God?
Q.F3. Why didn't the Buddha answer questions about the existence of God?
Q.F4. Why doesn't the Buddha return to save humanity?
Q.F5. Was the Buddha a real historical figure, or is he a constructed image?
Q.F6. Could the Buddha see the future?

## Q1.G. Rebirth and Past Lives

Q.G1. Does rebirth really exist, or is it only a belief?
Q.G2. Why do some people say they remember a past life?
Q.G3. How does Buddhism explain people who claim to see their "past lives"?
Q.G4. After death, does a person still remember their past life?

## Q1.H. Offerings and Rites for the Dead

Q.H1. Does burning votive paper offerings actually give the deceased money?
Q.H2. Does donating money to a temple really generate merit?
Q.H3. Can making offerings for the dead change their karma?
Q.H4. Does chanting sutras help the deceased find liberation?
Q.H5. Does reciting the Buddha's name at the moment of death really help the dying person?

## Q1.I. Ethics and Doing Good

Q.I1. Is doing good deeds in order to become wealthy a form of greed?
Q.I2. If someone does a great many good deeds in order to gain merit, is it still "good"?
Q.I3. Can a person with no religious belief who lives a good life still be considered meritorious?
Q.I4. Why do good people still meet with misfortune, while bad people live in comfort?
Q.I5. What karmic consequence does a person who dies by suicide face?
Q.I6. Do good and evil really exist, or are they only human concepts?

## Q1.J. Emptiness and Worldview

Q.J1. Does emptiness (śūnyatā) mean that "nothing exists"?
Q.J2. If the mind creates the world, does the external world really exist?
Q.J3. If there is no more "self" after awakening, is there still love and compassion?
Q.J4. If everything is impermanent, what meaning does love have?
