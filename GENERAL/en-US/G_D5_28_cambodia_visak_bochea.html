G.D5.28. Cambodia: Visak Bochea — Vesak
Full moon of the sixth Buddhist month (Visakha)

Visak Bochea is the Khmer name for Vesak (Pali: Vesākha Pūjā), the observance of the three great events in the Buddha's life: birth, awakening, and final passing. Its core meaning is explained in G.D5.3, in the Vietnamese calendar group. In Cambodia, it falls on the full moon of the sixth Buddhist month, the month of Visakha, and is a national public holiday.

1. What is the full moon of the sixth Buddhist month?

It is the full moon of the month of Visākha, the month traditionally associated with the Buddha's three great life events (G.D5.3). Thailand, Laos, and Cambodia designate this same full moon as the sixth Buddhist month; Vietnam records it as the fifteenth day of the fourth lunar month (G.D5.3). When an exact Gregorian date is needed, check the particular year (G.D5.1).

2. Visak Bochea in Cambodia

Three layers for understanding Visak Bochea in Cambodia

  • Foundational meaning: the Buddha's birth, awakening, and final passing, and the lessons they convey (G.D5.3); this core is the same throughout Theravāda countries.
  • Shared practice: temple visits, Dhamma listening, offerings, and undertaking the eight precepts (Pali: aṭṭhasīla) for one day and night. In the evening, people make a candle procession around the temple, circling three times with candles, incense, and flowers—a shared Theravāda custom (G.D5.8).
  • Custom: the day is a national public holiday. Large observances are held at major temples and, in some years, at Oudong Hill, where royal and monastic relic stupas stand. The scale and venue vary from year to year and belong to the organisational layer.

3. What newcomers should know

  • The full-moon evening, when the candle procession takes place, is the most solemn time.
  • Offer according to your means. If you are in Cambodia during the festival, ask permission before taking photographs in temple grounds.
  • For the festival's teaching, read the core article: G.D5.

Glossary:

  • Visak Bochea (Khmer): the Khmer name for Vesak; its core meaning is explained in G.D5.
  • Vesak / Vesākha (Pali): the name of a month in the ancient Indian calendar, traditionally associated with the Buddha's three great life events.
  • Bochea (Khmer, from Pali pūjā): “paying homage”; introduced in G.D5.
  • Oudong (Khmer): a hill with stupas associated with Cambodia's royal and monastic communities, about 35 km northwest of Phnom Penh.
  • Eight precepts (Pali: aṭṭhasīla): eight training rules observed for one day and night (G.D3).
  • Buddhist Era (BE): a calendar era traditionally reckoned from the Buddha's final passing.

Source notes
