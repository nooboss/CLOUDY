G.E7.7. Vietnam: Amitābha Buddha Commemoration Day
Seventeenth day of the eleventh lunar month

This day honours Amitābha Buddha, traditionally observed in Vietnam on the seventeenth day of the eleventh lunar month. Amitābha is the buddha of the Western Pure Land, Sukhāvatī, and a central focus of East Asian Pure Land practice (G.E2, G.E4).

1. Who is Amitābha Buddha?

Amitābha means “Infinite Light”; the related name Amitāyus means “Infinite Life.” Pure Land sutras describe his vows and Sukhāvatī as a favourable environment for practice. Amitābha should not be understood as a creator god. Pure Land practice joins faith, vows, and practice: trust in the path, aspiration for rebirth in the Pure Land, and ethical life and Buddha-recollection.

2. Why this date?

The seventeenth day of the eleventh lunar month is a Vietnamese devotional calendar date. It is not presented as an independently established historical birthday; other countries and communities may use different dates or no comparable annual observance.

Buddha-recollection and the day's meaning

Temples and homes may recite “Namo Amitābha Buddha,” chant Pure Land sutras, undertake precepts, and dedicate merit. Recitation is mind training, not a formula for luck: it repeatedly directs attention toward awakening, compassion, and ethical conduct (G.E6).

What newcomers should know

  • Listen, recite softly if comfortable, and follow the temple's rhythm.
  • Do not mistake a Pure Land service for a transaction promising rewards without practice.
  • Offerings are voluntary; kindness and a stable daily practice are the best participation.

Summary and next steps

  • The day honours Amitābha on the seventeenth day of the eleventh lunar month in Vietnam.
  • Its centre is Pure Land practice: faith, vows, and wholesome action sustained through Buddha-recollection.

Glossary:

  • Amitābha: “Infinite Light,” the Buddha of the Western Pure Land.
  • Sukhāvatī: Amitābha's Pure Land.
  • Pure Land practice: faith, vows, and practice centred on Amitābha.

Source notes
